EnzoBot — The Entity

One page: § 1 what changes for you · § 2 how it works · § 3 the plan.

Goal: an intelligence that has already read your whole digital world, never forgets, exists as one continuous being across every machine — and works your night shift: at 22:00 it plans tonight's work and checks quota, you confirm with one tap; while you sleep it reviews PRs and analyzes your tasks; at 06:00 the results — and its plan for your day — are waiting. Easy whitelisted tasks run at midnight without even asking. And it becomes you, day by day: every night it studies how you actually decided and edits itself closer to your judgment. The inversion from the film: it serves you, its beliefs are inspectable, outward actions always wait for your tap, and you hold the kill switch.

§1 What changes for you

Before — every morning today

  • Walk Gmail → Slack → GitHub → Jira, channel by channel
  • Hold it all in your head, decide the next hour
  • Review requests wait until you find them
  • Things you meant to get back to… quietly slip
~30–45 min before real work starts

After — it's already done

  • 08:30 brief is waiting: one ranked list with reasons
  • Tap ✅ / 💤 / ▶ — triage in seconds
  • Review requests arrive already drafted — tap 📤 to post
  • Nothing slips: every open loop is tracked until you close it
~2–5 min, straight to the decision

§2 How it works

One mind, many hands

  • The mind is one long conversation, not one long process. Your DM maps to a single resumable Claude Code session. tmux may die overnight; the next wake does claude --resume — Day 30's brief knows what you snoozed on Day 29. /exit is the only deliberate amnesia.
  • Decisions are its turns. Something wakes it (scheduler, your DM, a button, an interrupt) → it reads memory + runs collectors → reasons in context → the decision is what it does: agenda updates, a brief, a draft, or silence.
  • Hands are parallel, the person is one. Thread sessions and Mac review panes share the same persona/rules and report everything back into memory — ask "how's the incident going?" and the mind answers from its own live hands.
  • VPS = signal (stable network, light judgment) · Mac = muscle (heavy jobs, your repos, your gh). Queue in between: sleep-proof, no ports on your laptop, no write creds on the VPS.

Four memory layers

World

agent-mem graph

Who/what relates to what — Slack, Jira, PRs, incidents. Live today.

Episodes

agent-mem flat

What every session did, auto-summarized via hooks. Live today.

Identity

directives Plan A

Persona + rules you teach it; synced + injected into every session, every machine.

Open loops

agenda Plan B

What's pending for you. Personal DMs are read live, never stored.

The leash (never changes without your say)

  • Night autonomy is plan-scoped: the 22:00 batch you approved + your easy-task whitelist — nothing else runs unattended
  • Never posts, replies, approves, or merges on its own — outward actions happen only behind your explicit tap, using your own auth
  • Never stores your personal DMs / private-channel content
  • Never self-deploys code — self-fixes arrive as PR suggestions
  • Every belief inspectable: agenda rows, rules, memory — all readable files/tables
